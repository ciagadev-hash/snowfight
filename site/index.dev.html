Grow

Roll over snow piles to pack on snow. More snow means a bigger ball, a higher price and harder shoves.

Shove

A dash launches you forward and sends whoever you hit flying. It recharges in about a second. Bigger balls hit harder.

Knock them off

Push a ball into the sea within a few seconds of hitting it and 60% of its snow becomes yours.

The ice

Every round the floe cracks and shrinks, then refreezes. The pink dashed line shows where it breaks next.

Spawn shield

You roll in with a few seconds of shield. Shoves bounce off it. Dashing drops it early.

Cash out

From $8, hold cash out for 3 seconds and stand still. Any bump resets it. Done and the money is yours.

What is my snowball worth?

The prize pot is spread over all the snow in play. Your price is your share of it, live: your snow ÷ all snow × the pot. Grow, or take someone else's snow, and the number goes up.

FAQ
Is it free to play?

Yes. You only need a Solana wallet (Phantom, Solflare or Backpack) to sign in.

Does connecting cost anything?

No. Connecting shares your public address only. SnowPump never asks you to sign a payment to play.

When do payouts start?

When $SNOWPVP launches. Until then every round is a warm-up, and your best runs are saved on your device.

What happens if I fall in?

You lose your snowball. If someone shoved you, they keep 60% of your snow and the rest goes back to the pot.

Why did my cash out reset?

Any bump while you hold resets it. Find a quiet spot, ideally away from bigger balls.

Who else is on the floe?

Right now you roll with practice bots, marked BOT. Live multiplayer opens with the launch.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Use the stick to roll and the buttons to shove and cash out.
